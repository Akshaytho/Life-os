# Life OS — Product Requirements Baseline

## North Star

Life OS is a private, mobile-first personal operating system that externalizes memory, preserves chosen direction, supports deliberate capability building, and makes returning from drift easy.

It is not a generic habit tracker, productivity scorecard, or autonomous AI life manager.

## Core Problems

1. Important decisions and reasons are forgotten over time.
2. Mood, inspiration, comparison, relationships, setbacks, and new possibilities can cause direction drift.
3. Real life commitments must change what is reasonable to expect from a day.
4. Progress toward a future travel life requires capability development, not only planning.
5. AI should understand accumulated context without treating every old conversation as equally true.

## Current Direction

Build the financial and creative foundation that enables long-term travel and exploration while continuing the software job during the foundation period.

Current supporting pillars:

- Job / financial stability
- Creator skill development
- Content as deliberate practice and evidence
- Health
- Travel readiness over time

## Becoming Journey

The first active journey is **Travel Creator**.

The initial skill phase is **Sound Design**. Future skills exist as a map, not simultaneous commitments.

Progress is measured through evidence such as learned, practised, applied, reviewed, and repeated—not invented mastery percentages.

## V1 Navigation

- Today
- Journey
- Calendar
- Memory
- You

AI, Brain Dump, Drift, Capture, Notifications, and External Intelligence are cross-cutting overlays rather than mandatory permanent navigation destinations.

## Today

Today answers: **What matters today given the life I actually have today?**

It combines:

- Current direction
- Calendar reality
- Current journey/skill
- Intentionally small daily focus
- Quick capture
- Drift access
- Evening review

A family event, medical appointment, time with friends, or deliberate rest is not automatically a productivity failure. Life OS should evaluate the day against intentional reality, not an imaginary perfect schedule.

## Calendar

Calendar events include category and commitment level so Life OS can reason about constraints.

Initial categories:

- Work
- Creator
- Learning
- Health
- Family
- Friends
- Travel
- Personal
- Rest

Initial commitment levels:

- Fixed
- Important
- Flexible
- Optional

## Journey

Journey answers: **What am I becoming capable of?**

A skill contains techniques, practice sessions, evidence, learnings, and related reels.

## Reels

A reel is both creative output and training evidence. Life OS stores intention, techniques, user reflection, related learnings, and later external AI observations.

External AI observations never replace the user's own reflection.

## Brain Dump

Capture must be nearly frictionless. Raw input is preserved before AI classification. Classification may include goal, idea, problem, emotion, person, concern, task, learning, travel, content, career, diet, or NOT NOW.

## Drift

A global “I’m Drifting” action records what is happening and helps distinguish temporary inspiration, comparison, avoidance, emotional reaction, or genuine reconsideration.

The target is not zero drift. The target is reliable return.

## NOT NOW

New ideas are captured without becoming active projects. Repeatedly returning ideas can later be reviewed deliberately.

## Reviews

End-of-day review is not a percentage. It focuses on:

- What mattered
- What moved forward
- What did not and why
- What was learned
- Whether the user returned after drift
- Anything worth preserving

## Memory

Memory is layered:

1. Structured current truth
2. Historical domain events
3. Semantic retrieval
4. Derived summaries/patterns

Vector similarity is never allowed to overrule explicit current state or active decisions.

## AI

AI supports Ask, Reflect, Decide, Review, Reset, Plan, and Challenge style interactions, but context retrieval is intent-aware and minimal.

AI output categories must stay distinguishable from facts and user decisions.

## ChatGPT / External Intelligence

ChatGPT will eventually connect through scoped MCP tools. Initial access is read-only except controlled external-analysis submission.

A user may bring a reel to ChatGPT for deeper analysis. The resulting observations enter an External Intelligence Inbox with provenance and approval rather than directly rewriting canonical state.

## Automation

Life OS background jobs may consolidate daily/weekly events and produce attention candidates. External scheduled review should usually remain silent; it should interrupt only when action is genuinely useful.

## Product Boundary

Life OS is not a mental-health diagnostic system or a replacement for qualified professional care. It may record user-reported states and behavioral patterns, but derived interpretations remain explicitly non-diagnostic.

## Build Principle

Build the smallest version that becomes useful in daily life, then expand from real usage. Architecture should permit future capability without forcing every future capability into V1.
